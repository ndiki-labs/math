# Changelog

## 27 November 2026

### Ramsey number of the 9-cycle

- Added Lean proofs for the numerical block budget, the reduction to a minimal dense induced graph, and the path lemmas that close a 9-cycle.
- Verified the census of 1,094 encodings for the $K_5$ case in Lean. Connecting the census and the exclusions to all candidate graphs remains a proof obligation.
- Added the upper-bound composition theorem, with five explicit hypotheses still to prove. The Lean proof of the lower bound is complete and the formalization of the upper bound remains partial. [Lean composition theorem](erdos-556-ramsey-c9/lean/RamseyC9/UpperBound.lean)
- Documented the status of the formalization in both editions of the manuscript and the audit. The working manuscript presents the computer-assisted proof of the equality. Independent mathematical review is pending. [English manuscript](erdos-556-ramsey-c9/MANUSCRIPT.md) · [English audit](erdos-556-ramsey-c9/AUDIT.md)
